Diagnosis and hand-off status: the supplied reference is a 960x540 pixel-art pool scene whose authored backdrop is `hh_room_pool` member `bg1` (`02316_bg1.png`) from the `pool_a` room definition. The pool water is runtime member `vesi1` (cast member 2465), filled with `#009C9C`, rendered with Director `MASK` ink and blend 60; its separate immediately following mask member is `vesimask1` (2466), a black/white 489x217 bitmap. The old baker incorrectly converted the cyan source pixels themselves to luma alpha and then applied blend again, which made the pool too white and exposed the wrong blue/white edge. The shared mask path now resolves and decodes the paired cast mask for authored and live sprites, applies black-reveal/white-hide alpha to the source, and leaves blend to the frame compositor; focused ink/renderer tests pass, the debugger target builds, the full configured CTest suite passes, and an actual `hh_room_pool.cct` probe through the committed `SpriteBaker` and `SoftwareFrameRenderer` resolves both members, renders the pool at the score registration-derived position, reports 73,656 opaque and 31,968 transparent baked water pixels, and produces the reference-region blended values (`#66C3C3` over white tile, `#007186` over the dark pool wall, and `#299AAF` over the blue pool edge) without the rectangular white halo or an extra blue border. Network tracing found a separate general bootstrap issue: before the first `fuse_client.cct` response arrives, 81 identical external-cast HTTP tasks are enqueued for the same resource, starving movie initialization. `QueuedNetProvider` now coalesces pending external CCT/CST requests by their resolved URL/fallbacks, with a regression assertion that duplicate pending cast requests reuse one task; this is committed as `86423456` (`Coalesce pending external cast fetches`).
